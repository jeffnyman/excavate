# Portal: a path through the story

*Portal* has no puzzles to solve and no way to lose. The story is told in pieces scattered
across Worldnet's dataspaces, and reading some pieces unlocks others. This is a path through
it, level by level, worked out from the game's own data: what each piece needs read before
it unlocks, as the game checks it. How that was found is in [portal.md](portal.md#the-story).
Excavate has played it through, from the login to the last message, by following these rules.

## How to read something

- On the map, move to a dataspace and press Space to go in. Its list shows what's there to
  read, with a tick by what you've read. The highlight starts on the first unread item.
- Space chooses the item, and Space again (ENTER DATABASE) opens it.
- It counts as read only once you've paged to its end. Up highlights MORE, Down turns the page,
  and the title across the top changes to NO MORE on the last page. Then Space, and Space again
  on EXIT THIS LEVEL, takes you back to the list.
- To leave a dataspace, go Down to the row of buttons, Right to the square at the right end
  (EXIT TO INTERFACE), and press Space.

A few things to know:

- **Homer**, on the map's bottom right, is where his messages are read. At the start of
  levels 1, 2 and 4 he shows a menu of kinds of story instead of a list: Up into it, Space,
  then Down until CURRENT is at the top, Space to choose it, Down to ENTER DATABASE, and Space.
  After that his dataspace is a list like the others. The steps below count his messages; his list names them by a code and
  a time (NARR1 01062106@152301 and so on).
- **PsiLink and Military** demand a DNA code. Any key does: the game lets you in whenever
  there's something there for you to read.
- **Some things happen by themselves.** Once the six pieces of level 1's first step are read,
  a "transit query" ends and the game takes you to Homer. The last message of the game appears
  straight after History's last page.
- **The disk** for each level is asked for once, as the level starts. Excavate puts it in for
  you.
- **Extra reading** unlocks along the way: SciTech's articles on interfaces, History's
  timelines by decade, and more. None of it is needed, so it's left out of the steps.

Within a step, the order doesn't matter. Each step needs everything in the steps before it.
